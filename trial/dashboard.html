Do prices move toward the forecast?

Measured from Kalshi’s price history at each afternoon settle, once a market’s day has started. Nothing measured yet.

Compares each market’s mid price when we scanned with its mid when the market’s day started, in points toward the forecast (below zero means away). A noisy evening price tends to drift back toward any sensible forecast, so movement toward the forecast alone does not prove it knows more; the money tests decide.

Same forecasts, blend, 8-point edge and size limits as the main bot, on its own hypothetical $25. Instead of paying the ask it rests a limit order 1¢ better than the best bid (or joins the best bid when the spread is 1¢) and cancels it when the market’s day starts. It counts as filled only if Kalshi’s minute-by-minute history shows a trade or an offer reaching that price after the order, or going through it when the order only joined the queue. Makers pay no Kalshi fee on weather markets. Read the fill rate with the win rate: resting orders tend to fill when the price moves against them.
