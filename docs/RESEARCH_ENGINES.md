# Strategy engines and their status (2026-10-22)

Everything new is research-only or paper-only. Nothing here can reach live.py. live.py copies only v3 rain paper
trades: to Kalshi's demo exchange (fake money) any time, and to real money only on or after 2026-11-11 after the user's
own typed unlock. The gate verdict is shown at unlock but does not block it. The registry (registry.py) is the source
of truth for each strategy's lifecycle state, and the dashboard's control center shows it.

| Engine | File | Lifecycle | What it does now | What it does not do yet |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Decision audit ledger | audit.py (+ strategy.py, shadow.py) | in force | One append-only row per market evaluated (quote, model inputs and their age, rules hash, action, reason code, selector verdict); trades and their decision rows are written together or not at all; settlements and fills are added as events | Records nothing for live.py orders (live.db keeps its own ledger) |
| Strategy registry | registry.py | in force | Ids, versions, lifecycle states, modes; may_trade_live() is false for every strategy; an append-only state log in paper.db | Changing a state is a reviewed code change, not a button |
| Meta selector (accuracy first) | selector.py | forward test, shadow | Beside every v3 candidate: abstains unless data is fresh, the spread is acceptable, model and market agree within 30 points, the risk cluster has room, there are 30+ settled markets in the probability range, the conservative (lower 95%) win chance is at least 60%, and EV after fees on that conservative chance is at least 1c | Never trades; thresholds are frozen per experiment (accuracy_first.v1) |
| Calibration | calibration.py | in force (measurement) | Brier, log loss, ECE, reliability bins with Wilson intervals | Breakdowns beyond v3 rain (needs settled history in other strategies) |
| Risk clusters, capital efficiency | risk.py | in force (measurement) | weather:date:region clusters, exposure per cluster, profit per dollar per day | Cluster caps on v3 (frozen); the cap lives in the shadow selector |
| Fees and incentives | fees.py | in force for new engines | Taker and maker fees by series fee type; incentives counted only when INCENTIVES_ELIGIBLE (False until the user confirms) | Verified maker fee on maker-fee series |
| Market structure | research/structure.py | forward test, descriptive | Threshold ladders, mutually exclusive and exhaustive bucket baskets at executable prices after fees, confirmed in the live order book | Synthetic replication beyond ladders and baskets; executing baskets (never) |
| Resolution certainty | resolution.py | research | States UNCERTAIN to SETTLED from the settlement station's running extreme, with a margin, revision risk and source-mismatch guard | A live observation feed (PLAN3 test A showed decided markets already cost $1.00) |
| Price convergence | control_view.py (+ marks.py) | research | Open trades at Kalshi bids, best and worst moments since entry, a research hold or exit note | Paper exit tests (need their own plan) |
| Metrics | metrics.py (+ control_view.py) | in force (measurement) | Per strategy: settled count, win rate with its count, profitable rate, net and per-contract P&L, realized vs expected, Brier, max drawdown, largest loss, holding time, turnover, profit per dollar per day, daily ratio (5+ days), worst risk cluster, and daily P&L correlation between strategies | Fill rate and adverse selection live in the maker and L reports |
| Post-mortems | postmortem.py | in force (measurement) | One or more loss-cause codes per settled trade (timing, stale data, wrong settlement source, calibration, low-probability bet, normal variance) | Never changes a strategy by itself |
| Liquidity rewards and spread (PLAN5 L) | research/lip.py | idea, starts 2026-10-24 | Hourly order-book samples, reward share by the program's rule (prorated to the time before a fill), queue-aware fills, candle-mid adverse selection at 1 and 5 minutes, spread-only verdict, SE by day, event and no-edge | Second-by-second snapshots |
| Market discovery | research/discover.py | in force (catalogue) | Every open Kalshi market (single and combo) in its own database: daily series cache, paginated open pass, upserts by ticker, append-only lifecycle (open to closed or settled), rate limit, backoff, --every cache | Not a strategy |
| Order flow, fills, lead-lag | research/micro.py | research, exploratory | Records up to 12 markets' books and trades every 5 s on demand. Imbalance vs the later mid (5 s to 30 min); join, improve or cross with a queue-aware fill model; spread capture and adverse moves after fills; P(toxic fill) by state; lead-lag within events after costs | Needs the websocket (API key) for finer than 5 s and for cancellations; a full inventory-managing quoting simulator |
| Combos (multivariate) | research/combo.py | research, exploratory | Open combos' legs priced with a one-factor Gaussian copula: independence price, combo mid, implied correlation; after settlement, Brier of mid vs independence vs copula at an earlier-fitted correlation | RFQs and quotes (need an API key) |
| Favorite / longshot | research/longshot.py | research, exploratory | Win rate vs price by category, side and price tenth on K1's Kalshi trades, SE by market date | Runs only after K1 is scored; a forward test needs its own plan |
| Copy trading (PLAN4) | research/plan4.py | backtest | Polymarket P1/P2/P2c and Kalshi K1, each scored once | - |
| Station gap (PLAN5 S) | (not built) | idea | - | Needs Polymarket temperature history inside its 60-day window |
| External lead-lag (Polymarket, futures, releases) | (not built) | - | - | Synchronized external feeds; micro.py's recorder format is the place to add them |
