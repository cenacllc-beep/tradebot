## 3. Root causes, in order of impact

1. **No scan, no opportunity (infrastructure).** Ten of 15 market days had no scan, so the bot had no chance to find
   a trade on those days.
   - Since Oct 24 the laptop has run every job by itself, but it often had no network. On Oct 25 and Oct 26 the hourly
     jobs ran on time, yet every one found api.elections.kalshi.com unreachable for hours, and name resolution failed.
     That is what Windows Modern Standby with "Network connectivity in Standby" off looks like: jobs run, the network
     stays down.
   - The r59 settings script now reads that setting (read-only) and reports it. Only the user can change it.
   - A laptop-independent runner (cloud/README.md) would remove this cause entirely. It waits on the user.
2. **The rule finds few trades, and those few are where the forecast is weakest (strategy, not code).**
   - A trade needs the NWS forecast to differ from the market by at least 16 points plus the spread (50/50 blend and
     8-point edge, both fixed rules).
   - The market and the forecast usually agree, so 1-2 of about 35 markets qualify.
   - When they disagree that much, the market has been right 15 times in 17. So the trades the rule selects are the
     forecast's worst calls: adverse selection.
   - No code defect causes this. It is the honest answer to the test's question so far (CLAUDE.md section 2). Changing
     the thresholds to get more trades would weaken the test, and needs the user's explicit go-ahead.
3. **Settle crashed on one network error (code bug, fixed).**
   - On Oct 26 (name resolution) and Oct 27 (TLS handshake timeout), one failed lookup in strategy.settle raised
     before the commit. Every result found in that run was lost.
   - run.bat then skipped live.py sync, the shadow settle, learn.py and the backup.
   - Open trades stayed open, so they kept counting against the 15% exposure cap and the daily loss stop.
4. **Coverage gaps in the model's city tables (data, needs the user's decision).**
   - Rain: Detroit (DTW) and West Palm Beach (WPB) are listed by Kalshi but missing from v3's table, so 2 of about 35
     markets a day get "model unavailable".
   - Temperature (logged, not traded): 10 of the 14 KXHIGH series the bot asks for no longer exist under those names
     (Kalshi now uses names like KXHIGHNY and KXHIGHTSEA), and range buckets ("B" markets) are not modelled. So the
     temperature log covers 4 cities' threshold markets only.
   - Adding cities changes the frozen v3 model's population mid-test, so this is on the "stop and ask" list.
5. **Demo copies skipped (environment, not a bug).** The demo exchange often has no counterparty. Skipping is the
   correct fail-closed behaviour: the copy is immediate-or-cancel by rule and never rests. Demo fills say nothing
   about real-money fills.

## 4. Edge cases found

- A market voided or decided without "yes"/"no" would keep its paper trade open forever, holding exposure. Settle now
  prints a SETTLE WARNING for it. The trade is not changed automatically, because the refund rule is not verified.
- When the network is down, settle used to print a 60-line traceback per run. It now stops after 3 failed lookups in a
  row and prints one line.
- tests/test_ohlc.py started failing on Oct 26 because its "--every" check used the real clock. The test now pins the
  clock. The code under test was not wrong.
- The structure report's "found" counts gross gaps before fees. The dashboard already shows only the after-fee and
  live-book counts.
- When a scan runs after 9:25 PM, the 9:25 PM evening report misses it. A late report task was refused by the
  permission system (Oct 25). The 3:21 PM afternoon report covers it the next day.

## 5. Fixes in this release (r60, with r59)

- strategy.settle:
  - per-market retries (3 tries);
  - one bad market never stops the rest;
  - each settled trade is committed as it is found, and everything else at the end even on an error;
  - one lookup per market;
  - stops after 3 failures in a row;
  - exits 1 only when Kalshi could not be read at all;
  - a warning for a market decided without yes/no.

  Tests: tests/test_settle.py. The scan and every v3 decision are unchanged.
- tasks/install_schedules.ps1 (r59 plus this release):
  - the 8:55 PM task may wake the PC;
  - read-only reports of "Allow wake timers", "Network connectivity in Standby" and the sleep states.
- jobs.py (r59): it keeps the PC awake while a step runs, at most an hour, and for a few minutes after the evening
  start and between scan tries.
- tests/test_ohlc.py: the clock is pinned.
