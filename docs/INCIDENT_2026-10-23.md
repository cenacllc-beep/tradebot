## Root cause
1. **Until Oct 15**, scans ran only when a cloud Claude session drove the PC through Desktop Commander. From the Oct 15
   evening, those sessions were stopped by the account's weekly usage limit (CHANGELOG, Oct 22). Desktop Commander
   itself no longer starts: npm cannot rename its cache folder on the PC.
2. **On Oct 22** the routine moved to Windows Task Scheduler (tasks\\*.cmd), so no Claude session is needed. That only
   takes effect after one manual step, double-clicking tasks\\install_schedules.cmd, and that step has not been done.
   Nothing on the PC starts the bot, so nothing has run since.
3. **Nothing in the code blocks trading.** A rehearsal of tonight's first evening run on a copy of the real paper.db,
   with fake Kalshi and NWS answers, worked through every step:
   - the ledger migration, with its backup first;
   - the Oct 15 settlements;
   - the scan, which opened 4 paper trades on planted edges, all ledger rows written;
   - the dashboard;
   - freshness.

   The database integrity check is ok.

## Hidden failure modes found while tracing it (would have caused the next outage)
1. **No runs on battery.** schtasks.exe cannot set power options, so Windows' defaults apply (Task Scheduler schema,
   settingsType): DisallowStartIfOnBatteries = true and StopIfGoingOnBatteries = true. On this convertible laptop a
   schedule does not start while unplugged, and a running scan is killed when the charger is pulled out.
   **Fixed:** install_schedules.ps1 sets both to false.
2. **Missed runs are never made up.** StartWhenAvailable defaults to false, so a scan missed while the PC was off or
   asleep at 8:55 PM is gone, even if the PC is back at 9:30 PM.
   **Fixed:** StartWhenAvailable = true.
3. **A made-up run at the wrong hour would corrupt the test record.** With item 2 fixed, a run missed at 8:55 PM could
   start the next morning. paper.db's obs keeps the first snapshot of each market, and the gate's Brier score reads it.
   A morning scan would permanently replace the evening forecast-vs-price snapshot of the next day's markets, and open
   trades about 20 hours before their day instead of about 4.
   **Fixed:** evening.cmd scans only from 8:50 PM to 0:59 AM (scan_window.py). Outside that window it settles and logs
   "Scan skipped", and any other answer from the check also skips the scan (fail closed). The window opens at 8:50 PM,
   not earlier, because shadow.py logs the airport forecast once per market and counts it for its forward test only
   between 00:50 and 01:30 UTC. An earlier make-up scan would cost that test a market day (review). A scan by hand
   belongs in the same window, and the skip message says so.
4. **Two settles at once.** With item 2 fixed, the PC being off through both 2:51 PM and 8:55 PM makes both jobs start
   together. Both would settle, sync live.db and back up at the same moment.
   **Fixed:** settle.cmd and evening.cmd run each step through joblock.py. That is an operating-system file lock: a
   crashed job cannot leave it stale. Waits longer than 45 minutes give a JOB LOCK line and exit 75.
5. **A failed settle logged as success, and no time when PowerShell fails.** Fixed earlier today (r57): the done line
   keeps any failing step's exit code, and "no-time" lines still count.
6. **The installer's result is easy to miss.** It now checks every schtasks call, says plainly whether the settings
   were applied, writes logs\\schedules.txt and waits for a key.

## Checked, not a problem now
- **Daily loss stop after the outage.** The stop counts losses by the time they are recorded, so a backlog settling at
  once could pause trading for a day. The two open trades can lose at most $0.64 + $1.08 = $1.72, below the $2.50
  stop, so tonight's scan cannot be paused by them. The rule itself is a safety control and is unchanged; changing it
  is the user's call.
- **Exposure cap.** If tonight's settle failed, the two open trades ($1.72) would leave $2.03 of the $3.75 cap. That
  means fewer trades, not none.
- **live.py.** Nothing pending or mismatched in live.db. A failure there never stops the paper scan (run.bat continues).
- **Backups before the scan** (a failed backup stops the scan by design). Off-folder backups worked on Oct 14 and 15.
  They cannot be checked from here.

## Follow-up: the night of Oct 23 (read on the PC at 3:31 AM, Oct 24)
- **The 8:55 PM job started on time and ran its settle.** The scan failed two seconds later: `getaddrinfo failed` for
  api.elections.kalshi.com, so the PC had no network. Exit 1, and nothing retried.
- **Every hourly job from 12:20 to 3:20 AM failed the same way.** The research requests gave up, and the PC clock
  could not be measured.
- **Diagnosis:** the laptop was in standby with its network off. The scheduled jobs still ran on time, but had no
  internet. The Claude app's link to the PC dropped at the same time (9:20 PM).
- **3:30 AM:** evening.cmd was double-clicked. It settled, then logged 6 western markets: their day had not begun yet,
  and 59 others had. It opened 0 trades. Those 6 snapshots were taken at 3:30 AM instead of 8:55 PM. The airport
  forecast logged with them falls outside its 00:50-01:30 UTC window, so that forward test does not count them, by
  its own rule.

**Fix (jobs.py, 2026-10-24).** The three task files now only call jobs.py. It writes the start and done lines and every
step's output into logs\tasks.log itself, a line at a time, so a long wait never locks the log against the other jobs.
jobs.py:
- **Waits for Kalshi to be reachable** (skipped when Windows uses a proxy).
  - The evening job waits as long as the scan window is open, so waking the laptop at any time before 1 AM gets that
    night's scan with no click.
  - The settle waits up to an hour, the hourly job up to 10 minutes.
- **Retries a failed scan** up to 3 times, 5 minutes apart, inside the window only.
- **Runs every step under the job lock.** A scan asks the window again once it holds the lock, and never waits for the
  lock past 1 AM.
- **Applies the battery and missed-run settings by itself** (hourly job, install_schedules.ps1): once, and again when
  the script changes. No second double-click is needed.

The evening task's time limit is 6 hours, so it can wait until 1 AM; the settle's is 3 hours. Each job stops waiting
half an hour before its limit (jobs.BUDGET_S). freshness.py allows the evening job 6.5 hours and the settle 3.5 before
calling it stuck. The settings are changed in place, so a task you disabled stays disabled.

**What still needs the laptop.** It must be awake with internet at some point between 8:50 PM and 1:00 AM. In standby
with the network off, nothing on it can reach Kalshi. Running the paper bot without the laptop needs the cloud runner
(cloud/README.md), which waits on a GitHub repository from the user.
