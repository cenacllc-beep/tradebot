# Audit of the research, detection and calibration system (2026-11-26, about 19:30 La Paz)

User: "investigate/ audit our research, detection and calibration system to ensure the system is learning in real-time
and making accurate, intelligent suggestions". Read-only on the PC (copies of paper.db, live15.db, logs\tasks.log,
learning.md and freshness.md were analysed in the cloud session); the repository's GitHub jobs and data were checked
directly. Not financial advice.

## 1. Short answer
- **The machinery works and is current.** Every scheduled job on the PC and on GitHub ran on time or is running; the
  PC's freshness monitor reads "all current (17 checks)"; the backup AI's check reads 0 problems. Since Nov 11 only
  one market day (Nov 13) was missed by the evening scan.
- **It learns, but on purpose not "in real time".** Every model that makes a prediction is frozen before it is judged
  (v3, the airport forecast, PLAN7, PLAN8, PLAN9, PLAN15, PLAN20). New data flows in every hour and every evening and
  is scored at fixed, pre-registered looks. A model that re-fitted itself after every result would chase noise and
  look good on paper while losing money; that is the failure mode the whole project is built to avoid. "Learning"
  here means: collect forward data continuously, judge each idea once on data it has not seen, keep what passes,
  drop what fails, and write a new version (with its own test) for any idea the data suggests.
- **What the data says today: nothing beats Kalshi's price yet.** Every forecast and every betting rule measured so
  far is less accurate than the market price or loses about the fee. The system's suggestions are therefore "do not
  bet more" until a forward test passes. That is the accurate suggestion, not a malfunction.

1. **The strategy registry (dashboard control center) was out of date** (fixed, registry.py, display only): it still
   showed v3 as awaiting its gate (the look failed on Nov 11), PLAN5 L as an idea (scored not testable Nov 25), and it
   listed none of PLAN7-PLAN20. All 13 plans are now listed with their real state; nothing in it can enable trading
   (may_trade_live stays false for every entry; tests pass).
2. **Two frozen plans were never implemented: PLAN11 (radar/gauge replay) and PLAN14 (skill vs luck on sports and
   crypto).** Both are past-data tests and can still be run; listed as IDEA in the registry. Not started without the
   user's word, because each needs its own data downloads and runs.
3. **PLAN10 (HRRR) still waits for its single score on the PC** (needs about 17 GB free disk).
4. **A misleading PLAN7 log line**: "candles file was unreadable; moved aside" appears each day for the new day's
   file. The file does not exist yet, so nothing is moved and nothing is lost (the candles are fetched and saved;
   every day's file is present). Cosmetic. Not edited: research/plan7.py is frozen and PLAN8 pins its functions.
5. **PC network hiccups** (Kalshi connection resets, one PLAN5 L settle on Nov 26 04:20 gave up after retries; the
   hourly account snapshot sometimes fails and keeps the last copy). All retried on the next run; PLAN5 L's calls were
   removed from the hourly job in r94.
6. **One evening scan missed since Nov 11 (Nov 13).** The cloud trial copy covers missed PC evenings for PLAN6-style
   secondary readings only.
7. **The account's own trades**: of 86 Kalshi fills in the last 14 days, 73 were made outside the bot (cash $9.17,
   no open positions on Nov 26). The bot's loss stops cannot see or limit those.

## 5. What would make the suggestions "smarter" without fooling ourselves
- Keep the frozen-then-judged loop; it is what makes a future "yes" trustworthy.
- The next real decisions come at fixed dates: PLAN20's score (days), PLAN19 (from Dec 29), PLAN15 (from Jan 1),
  PLAN7/8/9 and the airport test (about mid-January). Each pass earns a forward paper test; each fail is dropped.
- When the data suggests a new idea (for example PLAN7's correction if it passes), it becomes a new version with its
  own test, never a silent change to a running model.
